4. Manner of Articulation: How?

The type tells us what happens to the air. Click any symbol to hear its sound.

Plosive

air is stopped, then let go

Nasal

air goes out through the nose

Fricative

air is pushed through a narrow gap

Affricate

a plosive, then a fricative (2 sounds)

Approximant

moves smoothly, no friction
